## 🖥️ After Effects — Previews & Views Cheat Sheet

### Playback & Preview
| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **Spacebar** | Start or stop preview playback |
| **Numeric Keypad 0** | RAM preview (video + audio) |

### Viewer & Panel Management
| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **Shift + /** *(Forward Slash)* | Fit composition to viewer / Maximize space |
| **~** *(Tilde)* | Maximize or restore the panel under the mouse cursor |

---

**Quick tips:**
- **Numpad 0** gives you a true RAM preview with audio; plain **Spacebar** plays back in real time but may drop frames and is audio-off by default.
- **Shift + /** is great for quickly fitting the whole comp in view — press it again in some contexts to zoom back to 100%.
- **~ (Tilde)** is one of the most-used shortcuts for maximizing a panel under your cursor without changing your workspace layout — works on the Timeline, Project panel, Effect Controls, etc.
